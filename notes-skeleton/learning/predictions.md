# Predict, then verify

Before reading code or an explainer, write a prediction. Then check it. Calibration over time is the learning signal.
Hit = right location and mechanism; Partly = right area, wrong mechanism; Miss.

| Date | Item | Question | My prediction | Actual (anchor) | Hit/Partly/Miss | Confidence (1-5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Monthly: hit rate per area, and whether high confidence correlates with hits (overconfidence is the thing to fix).
